02The Dataset
What it covers

One list, every threatened plant in the Philippines.

Under DENR Administrative Order 2026‑20, this is the country's official record of threatened vascular flora. The app turns that document into something you can actually search, filter, and browse — on the bus, in the field, or at a booth.

03Inside the App
Features

Built for real lookup, not a static PDF.

Every species carries taxonomy, threat status, CITES listing, distribution, and live external data — instantly filterable.

01 · FILTER

Deep filtering

By threat status, division, CITES appendix, growth habit, island group & sub‑island, endemism, and family — stackable, in real time.

02 · SEARCH

Accent-smart search

Typing "pungapong" still finds "pungápong." Matches scientific name, authority, common name, family, and CITES code.

03 · DETAIL

Full species panel

Scientific name, authority, threat & CITES badges, common name, copy‑citation, and similar species from the same family.

04 · LIVE DATA

GBIF integration

Live occurrence counts pulled from the Global Biodiversity Information Facility — global and Philippines‑specific.

05 · MAP CONTEXT

Distribution parsing

Raw locality strings are rendered as island → province hierarchies, with photo and uncertain‑locality flags.

06 · INSIGHT

Analytics dashboard

Seven charts — top families, threat breakdown, division & CITES donuts, growth habit, top islands, endemicity.

07 · LANGUAGE

English / Filipino

One tap switches all interface chrome to Filipino, while scientific names and codes stay correctly in Latin.

08 · EXPORT

Share & export

Shareable filtered URLs, CSV export, print stylesheet, and native share — take a filtered list with you.

09 · SPEED

Keyboard shortcuts

/ to search, Esc to close, ↑↓ to navigate — built for fast repeat lookup.

06Get It On Your Phone
No app store needed

Add it to your home screen — or just scan the code.

It's a website, not an app-store download — so there's nothing to approve, update, or run out of storage for. Follow the steps below, or scan the code to open it right now.

iOS

iPhone & iPad

Safari browser
  1. 1Open phthreatenedplantsmobile.vercel.app in Safari (must be Safari, not Chrome).
  2. 2Tap the Share icon — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. 3Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen.”
  4. 4Tap Add in the top right. The icon now opens full‑screen, like a real app.
AND

Android

Chrome browser
  1. 1Open phthreatenedplantsmobile.vercel.app in Chrome.
  2. 2Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. 3Tap “Add to Home screen” or “Install app.”
  4. 4Confirm by tapping Add / Install. Done — it's on your home screen.
QR

Scan to Open

Any camera app
phthreatenedplantsmobile.vercel.app
No typing, no sign‑up — your camera app recognises the link automatically.
On a laptop or desktop, just bookmark the page — or in Chrome/Edge, open the browser menu and choose “Install…” / “Create shortcut” to pin it like a native app.
