# Draft: request to Co's Digital Flora of the Philippines

**Not sent.** For the author to review, edit and send from their own address.

**To:** the CDFP maintainers (contact address on philippineplants.org)
**Subject:** Request for permission — condensed use of CDFP distribution records in a free reference tool

Dear CDFP team,

I am a botanist and the author of *Threatened Philippine Plants*, a free, non-commercial, single-page reference to the Updated National List of Threatened Philippine Plants (DAO 2026-20): https://dao202620.vercel.app

For each of the 1,237 listed taxa, the tool shows where it is recorded, as island and province-level locality strings (for example, "LUZON: Ilocos Norte"). That information is a condensed reading of the distribution given on CDFP's family and species pages. The tool credits Co's Digital Flora of the Philippines (Pelser, Barcelona & Nickrent, 2011–) in its About notice, states that CDFP is authoritative, and links to CDFP. It does not copy CDFP's text, images or keys.

Before I release this publicly, I would like to ask:

1. Do you permit this use — island- and province-level distribution condensed from CDFP records, shown with credit and a link?
2. Is there a citation or licence statement you would like me to use? I currently cite it as: Pelser, P.B., Barcelona, J.F. & Nickrent, D.L. (2011 onwards). Co's Digital Flora of the Philippines. www.philippineplants.org
3. Would you prefer that I remove the distribution column and link to CDFP only?

I will follow whatever you decide. Thank you for the work that makes this possible.
