Disclaimer

This application is an independent personal project developed and maintained in a personal capacity.

Source data

The species list, conservation categories, and legal classifications presented here are drawn from DAO 2026-20, an official issuance of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), Philippines. The developer does not claim authorship of the legal instrument or the species list it contains. Growth habit annotations reflect the developer's own professional verification and judgment, and are not an official DENR determination.

Distribution and photographs

Local distribution information has been compiled by the developer from Co’s Digital Flora of the Philippines (CDFP), and photographs linked from this tool are sourced from CDFP. Full credit for this data and imagery belongs to CDFP, Pelser et al. 2011–, and its contributors.

Accuracy and currency

This tool is provided as a reference only, without warranty of any kind. While care has been taken in compiling and presenting this information, no guarantee is made that the data is complete, current, or error-free.

Third-party data

Information sourced from external services (e.g., GBIF occurrence and taxonomic match data) is fetched live and is not independently verified. It reflects the state of those external databases at the time of viewing and may differ from official Philippine taxonomic determinations.
